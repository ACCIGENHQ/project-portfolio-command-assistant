# Project Financial Management

Practical, iterative AI prompts for Project Financial Management.

These prompts are tool-agnostic and designed to work with modern conversational AI tools. Each prompt guides you through the information required, allowing you to use your own business context, assumptions, and data.

## Available Quick Wins

1. Multi-Method Cost Estimation
2. Earned Value & Variance Analysis (EVA)
3. Scope Change Financial Impact Assessment
4. Financial Risk Matrix & Mitigation
5. Comprehensive Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA)
6. Return on Investment (ROI) Framework

## How to Use

Open a prompt, copy it into your preferred conversational AI tool, and follow the questions it asks. Provide only information you are authorized to share with that AI service.
